# 🚀 Startup Spotlight

> *Every great company started as an idea. This series is my journey to understand those ideas.*

## 📖 About This Series

As an aspiring **Full Stack Developer**, I believe learning to code is only one part of becoming a great engineer.

The other part is understanding the companies that build products used by millions of people.

**Startup Spotlight** is my personal collection where I explore innovative startups, their products, engineering decisions, and the problems they solve.

This is not just a list of companies—it's my learning journal.

---

## 🎯 Mission

My goal is to study one startup at a time and answer questions like:

- 💡 What problem does this startup solve?
- 🌍 Why does the world need it?
- 🛠️ What technologies power it?
- 📈 How does it make money?
- 🚀 Why do developers love it?
- 📚 What lessons can I learn from its journey?

---

## 🧩 What Each Spotlight Includes

Every startup profile follows a consistent format:

- 📌 Company Overview
- 🧠 Problem Statement
- 🚀 Products & Features
- 💻 Technology Stack
- 💰 Business Model
- 📈 Growth Story
- ⭐ Interesting Facts
- 🎓 What I Learned
- 💭 My Personal Takeaway
