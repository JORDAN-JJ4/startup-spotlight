# 🌍 What is Zerodha?

Zerodha is India's largest retail stock broker, offering a technology-first platform for investing in stocks, mutual funds, ETFs, commodities, and more.

The company disrupted the traditional brokerage industry by introducing **discount brokerage**, making investing more affordable for millions of Indians.

Today, Zerodha serves millions of investors and is known for its focus on simplicity, transparency, and technology.

---

# 🎯 The Problem It Solves

Traditional stock brokers often charged high brokerage fees and offered complicated trading platforms.

Investors faced challenges such as:

- High transaction costs
- Complex trading software
- Limited access to financial education
- Expensive investment services

Zerodha simplified investing with low-cost brokerage and an intuitive platform.
